Review and recovery

Resolve Needs attention items

Understand Cap Table alerts for expired awards, mature notes, valuation history, departed holders, equity plans, and 83(b) records.

Puter·Product documentation·2 min read
On this page
  1. Read the item before acting
  2. Use the action for the actual event
  3. Understand what clearing an item means
  4. Check the resulting records

Read the item before acting

Open Needs attention to review records whose dates or status call for action. Check the named holder, security, quantity, and date. An alert identifies a condition in the recorded data; it does not independently verify an agreement or establish that an obligation was fulfilled.

The Home dashboard shows a shorter attention list. Open the full page to review all items before a financing or report export.

Use the action for the actual event

Attention items and their follow-up
ItemWhat to review
Expired optionsCancel remaining lapsed options on the applicable date; review the plan destination. The item names which clock ran out: the grant reaching the end of its term, or a holder’s post-termination window closing. Multiple expired grants can be reviewed for bulk cancellation.
Departed holder’s awardsReview cancellation of unvested options or RSUs against the departure and prior events.
Expired warrantOpen the warrant record to review its exercises and cancellations, and inspect the agreement and any extension.
Matured noteRecord that maturity was dealt with or enter an agreed new maturity date.
83(b) recordRecord the actual filing date or that the election was not filed. The item appears once the 30-day window has closed with nothing recorded; the window itself is listed under upcoming events while it is open.
Valuation reminderRecord the provider’s new valuation after reviewing the relevant date. The reminder is raised by a valuation twelve months old or more, or by a priced round closing after it was set.
Expired planReview retirement or an actual extension of the plan’s expiration. Its unallocated shares still count as fully diluted until it is retired.
Class over its authorized sharesA class holds more issued shares than its authorized count. Raise the authorized shares under Share classes once the charter amendment is signed, rather than editing certificates to fit.

Understand what clearing an item means

Recording a note’s maturity as dealt with does not repay cash or remove the note from convertible models. Recording an 83(b) status does not file an election or make a late election timely. Changing an expiration should reflect a real amendment.

For a bulk cancellation, inspect the records and confirmation first. The operation takes a restore point and records the cancellations; it is not simply dismissing notifications.

Check the resulting records

After the action, inspect the security, plan balance, and History. Re-run any ownership view or report affected by the event. If an alert remains, check the condition it describes rather than repeating a transaction.

A clean attention list does not certify complete records or regulatory compliance. Some unsupported terms and missing source documents cannot be detected from the data alone.
